# Image Manifest for Story 42: The Cosmic Dance: Shiva Nataraja and the Universe
# Category: Mythology
# Duration: 60 minutes
# Difficulty: Intermediate

# Required Images:

concept.webp
- Description: The Nataraja statue with cosmic symbolism
- Visual elements: Shiva as Nataraja dancing within a ring of fire (Prabha Mandala), four arms holding drum and flame, one hand raised in blessing, foot raised in liberation, standing on the dwarf Apasmara
- Colors: Deep cosmic blues and purples, golden fire, bronze/copper tones for the figure, starlight accents
- Style: Majestic, sacred, cosmic—blending traditional iconography with universal cosmic imagery
- Mood: Awe, reverence, cosmic wonder, eternal rhythm

analogy.webp
- Description: Visual metaphor of the dance of creation and transformation
- Visual elements: A flowing dance between light and shadow, creation and dissolution—perhaps showing a figure dancing with one hand releasing stars and the other hand holding flame, surrounded by swirling cosmic energy
- Colors: Warm golds and oranges (creation/fire) blending with cool blues and silvers (cosmos/preservation)
- Style: Ethereal, flowing, dynamic movement captured in stillness
- Mood: Balance, harmony, the beauty of opposites dancing together

intro.webp
- Description: Cosmic dance imagery with swirling galaxies and energy
- Visual elements: Swirling galaxies, nebulae, and cosmic energy forming dance-like patterns; perhaps a subtle suggestion of a dancing figure within the cosmic swirls; stars and light trails suggesting movement and rhythm
- Colors: Deep space blues and purples, golden starlight, pink and orange nebula colors, white light accents
- Style: Cosmic, dreamlike, suggesting both vastness and intimacy
- Mood: Wonder, invitation, the universe as living dance
