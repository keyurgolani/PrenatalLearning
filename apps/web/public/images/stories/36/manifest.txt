# Image Manifest for Story 36: The Wood Wide Web: Forest Whisperers
# Category: Nature
# Duration: 60 minutes
# Difficulty: Intermediate

# Required Images:

concept.webp
  - Description: Abstract representation of mycelium network
  - Content: Intricate web of glowing fungal threads (mycelium) spreading underground, 
    connecting tree roots in a vast network. Bioluminescent quality with soft golden 
    and silver threads against rich dark soil. Should evoke the feeling of a living 
    internet beneath the forest floor.
  - Style: Ethereal, scientific yet magical, warm earth tones with luminous accents
  - Mood: Wonder, interconnection, hidden complexity

analogy.webp
  - Description: Visual metaphor - family as root system
  - Content: A family tree visualization where human figures are connected by 
    root-like structures underground, mirroring how trees connect through fungal 
    networks. Could show a mother figure as a central "Mother Tree" with smaller 
    figures (children) connected to her through glowing root networks.
  - Style: Warm, nurturing, blend of natural and human elements
  - Mood: Love, connection, support, belonging

intro.webp
  - Description: Welcoming forest scene
  - Content: A serene, ancient forest with towering trees, dappled sunlight filtering 
    through the canopy, soft moss-covered ground. One magnificent old tree (Mother Tree) 
    should be prominent. The scene should feel peaceful and inviting, with a hint of 
    magic in the quality of light. Subtle suggestion of the hidden world below through 
    gentle glowing at the base of trees.
  - Style: Photorealistic with dreamy quality, rich greens and golden light
  - Mood: Peace, wonder, invitation to explore, ancient wisdom

# Optional Supporting Images:

mother-tree.webp
  - Description: Close-up of a majestic Mother Tree
  - Content: A grand, ancient tree with massive trunk and spreading roots visible 
    above ground. Surrounding smaller trees and saplings. Warm, nurturing presence.

underground-network.webp
  - Description: Cross-section view of forest underground
  - Content: Cutaway illustration showing tree roots connected by fungal threads, 
    with nutrients (shown as glowing particles) flowing between trees.

seedling-receiving.webp
  - Description: A small seedling receiving nutrients
  - Content: Tender image of a tiny seedling in shade, with visible connection to 
    larger tree through glowing underground threads. Sugar/nutrients flowing toward it.
