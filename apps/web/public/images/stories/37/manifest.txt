# Image Manifest for Story 37: The Water Cycle: The Journey of a Drop
# Category: Nature
# Duration: 60 minutes
# Difficulty: Foundational

## Required Images

### concept.webp
Abstract representation of the water cycle
- Visual elements: Circular flow showing evaporation, condensation, precipitation, collection
- Color palette: Blues, whites, soft greens
- Style: Ethereal, flowing, interconnected
- Key imagery: Sun rays lifting water, clouds forming, rain falling, rivers flowing to ocean
- Mood: Eternal, peaceful, cyclical

### analogy.webp
Visual metaphor for water's resilience and persistence
- Visual elements: Water droplets slowly carving through stone, creating a canyon
- Color palette: Deep earth tones with flowing blue water
- Style: Powerful yet gentle, showing contrast between soft water and hard stone
- Key imagery: Grand Canyon-like formation with water flowing through
- Mood: Patient strength, gentle persistence, transformation over time

### intro.webp
A raindrop on a leaf - the opening scene
- Visual elements: Single crystal-clear raindrop resting on a green leaf after rain
- Color palette: Fresh greens, crystal clear water, soft morning light
- Style: Intimate, inviting, wonder-inducing
- Key imagery: Macro view of raindrop with light refracting through it
- Mood: Peaceful, curious, beginning of a journey

## Image Specifications
- Format: WebP
- Recommended dimensions: 1200x800 pixels minimum
- Aspect ratio: 3:2 preferred
- File size: Optimize for web (under 200KB each)
- Alt text should describe the image for accessibility

## Usage Notes
- concept.webp: Used in the core content section to illustrate the water cycle
- analogy.webp: Used to reinforce the lesson about water's patient persistence
- intro.webp: Used at the beginning of the story to set the scene
