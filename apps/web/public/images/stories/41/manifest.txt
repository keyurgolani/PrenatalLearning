# Image Manifest for Story 41: The Silk Road: The World's Ribbon
# Category: History
# Duration: 60 minutes
# Difficulty: Intermediate

# Required Images:

concept.webp
- Description: Abstract representation of the Silk Road trade routes
- Visual elements: A network of golden paths stretching across a stylized map, connecting East and West
- Colors: Warm golds, deep blues, rich reds representing different cultures
- Style: Artistic, flowing lines suggesting connection and movement
- Mood: Wonder, connection, ancient wisdom

analogy.webp
- Description: Visual metaphor of a tapestry woven from many threads
- Visual elements: A beautiful tapestry being woven, with threads of many colors (gold, blue, green, red, purple) interweaving to create a unified pattern
- Colors: Rich, diverse palette representing different cultures
- Style: Warm, artistic, showing both individual threads and the unified whole
- Mood: Unity in diversity, beauty through collaboration

intro.webp
- Description: Camel caravan crossing the desert at dawn/dusk
- Visual elements: Silhouettes of Bactrian camels with riders, bells visible on their harnesses, golden sand dunes, warm sky
- Colors: Golden sands, warm orange/pink sky, soft shadows
- Style: Peaceful, evocative, slightly dreamlike
- Mood: Adventure, journey, the romance of ancient travel

# Optional Supporting Images:

cities.webp
- Description: Montage of Silk Road cities (Chang'an, Samarkand, Constantinople)
- Visual elements: Blue-tiled domes, ancient walls, bustling marketplaces
- Style: Artistic representation of architectural diversity

goods.webp
- Description: Collection of traded goods
- Visual elements: Silk fabric, spices in bowls, porcelain, glassware, precious gems
- Style: Still life arrangement, rich colors and textures

exchange.webp
- Description: Merchants from different cultures meeting and trading
- Visual elements: People in diverse traditional clothing, sharing goods and conversation
- Style: Warm, human connection, cultural diversity
