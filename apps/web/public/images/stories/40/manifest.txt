# Color Theory: Painting with Light - Image Manifest
# Story ID: 40
# Category: Art & Design

## Required Images

### concept.webp
Description: Abstract representation of the color spectrum and prism
Visual Elements:
- A crystal prism with white light entering from one side
- Rainbow spectrum emerging and spreading from the prism
- Colors should flow smoothly: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet
- Ethereal, magical quality with soft glowing edges
- Dark background to make colors pop
- Subtle sparkles or light particles in the spectrum
Style: Dreamy, luminous, scientifically inspired but artistically rendered

### analogy.webp
Description: Visual metaphor of emotions as colors on an artist's palette
Visual Elements:
- A heart-shaped or organic artist's palette
- Pools of vibrant colors representing different emotions
- Warm colors (red, orange, yellow) on one side
- Cool colors (blue, green, violet) on the other side
- Soft brushstrokes suggesting the blending of emotions
- Gentle, nurturing atmosphere
- Perhaps a subtle silhouette of mother and baby reflected in the colors
Style: Warm, emotional, painterly, soft edges

### intro.webp
Description: Sunrise bringing color to a black and white world
Visual Elements:
- Split composition: left side in grayscale, right side in full color
- Golden sunrise on the horizon as the dividing element
- Landscape elements (trees, flowers, sky) transitioning from gray to color
- The color spreading like a wave of warmth
- Soft, hopeful morning light
- Sense of awakening and wonder
Style: Magical realism, transitional, hopeful, dawn atmosphere

## Color Palette Guidelines
- Primary spectrum colors should be vibrant but not harsh
- Use soft gradients and glowing effects
- Maintain a sense of warmth and wonder appropriate for prenatal content
- Balance scientific accuracy with emotional resonance

## Technical Specifications
- Format: WebP
- Recommended dimensions: 1200x800 pixels minimum
- Aspect ratio: 3:2 preferred
- File size: Optimize for web (under 200KB each)
