The cosmic ocean of milk (Kshira Sagara) - a vast, luminous silver-white ocean stretching to the horizon under an ancient starlit sky
Mount Mandara as the churning rod - a majestic mountain rising from the ocean with Vasuki the rainbow-scaled serpent coiled around it
Devas and Asuras churning together - gods of light on one side and demons on the other, pulling the great serpent in cosmic cooperation
Lord Vishnu as Kurma - the divine tortoise beneath the waters, supporting Mount Mandara on his broad shell, providing the foundation
The emergence of Halahala - dark poison clouds rising from the churning ocean, threatening the cosmos with destruction
Lord Shiva drinking the poison - the great god consuming the Halahala, Parvati's hands at his throat, his neck turning the beautiful blue of Neelakantha
Lakshmi rising from the ocean - the radiant goddess of abundance emerging on a lotus flower, elephants showering her with sacred water, golden light surrounding her
Dhanvantari with the Amrita - the divine physician emerging from the waters holding the golden pot of the nectar of immortality, healing light emanating from him
The fourteen treasures displayed - Kamadhenu the wish-fulfilling cow, Ucchaisravas the divine horse, Airavata the white elephant, the Kalpavriksha tree, and precious gems arranged in celestial splendor
A mother and baby as ocean and treasure - a pregnant mother with hands on belly, visualized as the cosmic ocean with her baby as the precious treasure rising from the depths of love
