The battlefield of Kurukshetra - two vast armies facing each other at dawn, with Arjuna's magnificent chariot in the center, white horses standing ready, the scene both epic and contemplative
Arjuna's crisis - the great warrior slumped in his chariot, bow fallen from his hands, face showing deep anguish and confusion as he looks at the armies of his kinsmen
Krishna as charioteer - Lord Krishna holding the reins of Arjuna's chariot, his form radiating calm wisdom and divine love, blue-skinned with a gentle smile, ready to teach
The eternal soul - a luminous, radiant form of pure light representing the Atman, surrounded by the cycle of bodies it has worn through many lives, eternal and unchanging
Karma Yoga in action - hands offering work to the divine, symbolizing selfless action without attachment to results, with light flowing from the offering upward
The three Gunas - three interweaving streams of energy: golden Sattva (clarity and peace), red Rajas (passion and activity), and dark blue Tamas (inertia and rest), showing how they combine in all beings
The Vishvarupa cosmic form - Krishna revealing his universal form to Arjuna, containing all of creation within his body: suns, moons, galaxies, all beings, past present and future, overwhelming in its infinite scope
The paths of yoga - three intertwining paths leading to the same summit: the path of action (Karma), the path of devotion (Bhakti), and the path of knowledge (Jnana), each beautiful in its own way
Equanimity - a serene figure seated in meditation, unmoved by the storms of success and failure, pleasure and pain swirling around them while they remain centered and peaceful
The song of life - Krishna and Arjuna in the chariot as the sun rises over Kurukshetra, the teaching complete, Arjuna now standing tall with clarity and purpose, ready to fulfill his dharma
