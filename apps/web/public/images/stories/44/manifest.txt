Matsya the golden fish avatar - a magnificent golden fish with shimmering scales swimming through cosmic waters, saving the Vedas and guiding a boat through the great flood
Kurma the cosmic tortoise - Lord Vishnu as a giant tortoise beneath the ocean, supporting Mount Mandara on his broad shell during the churning of the cosmic ocean
Varaha the mighty boar - a powerful boar with tusks like crescent moons, rising from cosmic waters with the Earth goddess balanced on his tusks, rescuing her from the depths
Narasimha the man-lion - a fierce half-man half-lion figure bursting from a pillar at twilight, eyes blazing with protective love, defeating the demon Hiranyakashipu
Vamana the dwarf who became cosmic - a small Brahmin dwarf with wooden umbrella transforming into a cosmic giant, his three steps covering Earth, heaven, and the demon king's head
Parashurama the warrior sage - a fierce Brahmin warrior wielding his divine axe, standing for justice against tyranny, embodying righteous action
Rama the ideal prince - noble Prince Rama of Ayodhya with his bow, accompanied by Sita and Hanuman, embodying perfect dharma and the triumph of good over evil
Krishna the divine teacher - Lord Krishna on the battlefield of Kurukshetra, teaching the Bhagavad Gita to Arjuna, radiating wisdom and divine love
Buddha the enlightened one - the serene Buddha seated under the Bodhi tree, radiating compassion and peace, having attained perfect awakening
Kalki the future avatar - a majestic figure on a white horse against a dawn sky, sword raised, representing hope and the promise of dharma restored in the future age
