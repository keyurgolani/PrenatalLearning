# Image Manifest for Story 39: Stoicism: The Inner Fortress
# Category: Philosophy
# Duration: 60 minutes

# Required Images:

concept.webp
- Description: Abstract representation of the Inner Citadel - a fortress of peace within the mind
- Visual elements: A luminous fortress or citadel on a hill, surrounded by soft light, with a peaceful garden visible within its walls
- Style: Ethereal, peaceful, symbolic - representing inner strength and tranquility
- Colors: Soft golds, warm whites, deep peaceful blues, touches of green for the inner garden
- Mood: Serenity, strength, sanctuary, the unshakeable peace within

analogy.webp
- Description: Visual metaphor of a mountain standing still while a storm rages around it
- Visual elements: A majestic mountain peak, unmoved and serene, while dramatic storm clouds, lightning, and wind swirl around it
- Style: Dramatic yet peaceful contrast - the chaos of the storm vs. the stillness of the mountain
- Colors: Dark stormy grays and purples for the storm, solid earth tones for the mountain, perhaps a hint of golden light at the peak
- Mood: Groundedness, resilience, calm amidst chaos, unshakeable stability

intro.webp
- Description: Serene scene representing the calm center within chaos - the eye of the storm
- Visual elements: A figure in meditation or peaceful contemplation, surrounded by swirling elements (wind, leaves, abstract chaos) but in a bubble of perfect stillness
- Style: Contemplative, warm, showing the contrast between outer turbulence and inner peace
- Colors: Warm, peaceful tones at the center (golds, soft whites) transitioning to more turbulent colors at the edges
- Mood: Peace, centeredness, the quiet place within, accessible tranquility

# Optional Additional Images:

three-teachers.webp
- Description: Artistic representation of the three great Stoic teachers
- Visual elements: Stylized portraits or silhouettes of Seneca, Epictetus, and Marcus Aurelius, perhaps with symbolic elements (scroll, chains broken, crown)
- Style: Classical yet accessible, honoring the historical figures while remaining warm
- Colors: Warm earth tones, classical golds and deep reds
- Mood: Wisdom, timelessness, the continuity of philosophical insight

dichotomy-of-control.webp
- Description: Visual representation of the two circles of control
- Visual elements: Two overlapping or adjacent circles - one containing symbols of internal things (heart, mind, hands choosing), one containing external things (weather, crowds, clocks)
- Style: Clean, symbolic, educational yet beautiful
- Colors: Warm colors for the "within control" circle, cooler colors for "outside control"
- Mood: Clarity, understanding, the freedom that comes from knowing the difference

responding-vs-reacting.webp
- Description: The space between stimulus and response
- Visual elements: Abstract representation of a pause - perhaps a wave frozen mid-motion, or a breath held between inhale and exhale
- Style: Fluid, dynamic yet peaceful, capturing the moment of choice
- Colors: Flowing blues and whites, with a golden moment of stillness in the center
- Mood: Freedom, choice, the power of the pause
